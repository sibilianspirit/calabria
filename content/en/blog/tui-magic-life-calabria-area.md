TUI Magic Life Calabria sits in a pine wood on the Gulf of Sant'Eufemia, in the municipality of Pizzo, about 20 minutes' drive from Lamezia Terme airport. **Only the beach and the wood are within walking distance – the nearest town is 11 km away, so every trip beyond the resort means a car, a taxi or an organised excursion.** In return, the best stretch of Calabria's Tyrrhenian coast lies within an hour's drive: Pizzo, Tropea and Capo Vaticano. Below is a list of places with distances and drive times, plus a few spots the excursion coaches do not reach.

## Where the resort is {#where}

The club stands in a locality called Difesa, at the northern edge of the municipality of Pizzo in the province of Vibo Valentia. The SS18 trunk road runs right past it, and a belt of pine wood (*pineta*) separates the buildings from the sea – typical of the whole Gulf of Sant'Eufemia, whose flat hinterland was created by draining former marshland. The walk to the beach through the pines is about 450 metres. The beach is a mix of sand and shingle, so water shoes come in handy.

TUI opened its first Magic Life club in Italy here in 2019. The resort has more than 600 rooms and operates seasonally: according to TUI's websites, the 2027 season runs from 1 May to 24 October (as of November 2026).

Lamezia Terme airport is 16 km away, about a 20-minute drive. That is convenient at the start and end of the holiday, but there is a flip side: there is no town, promenade or shops nearby. The closest things are beach bars on the neighbouring beaches and a few roadside fruit stalls.

## Pizzo – fifteen minutes away {#pizzo}

[Pizzo](/destinations/pizzo/) is an old town on a tufa cliff above the sea. An afternoon and evening are enough, and there are three reasons to come.

**Tartufo.** The ball of hazelnut and chocolate ice cream with a liquid chocolate heart, rolled in cocoa, comes from here. You order it at the ice-cream parlours on Piazza della Repubblica, the main square with a terrace overlooking the gulf. We tell the story of the dessert in [Tartufo di Pizzo](/destinations/pizzo/tartufo-gelato/).

**Murat's castle.** In October 1815 Joachim Murat, Napoleon's brother-in-law and former King of Naples, was tried and shot in the small fortress next to the square. Inside is a museum with the cells and the courtroom – details on the [Castello Murat](/destinations/pizzo/castello-murat/) page.

**Piedigrotta church.** A grotto cut into the tufa right by the beach and filled with statues carved from the same rock. It lies before the town on the resort side, so the easiest thing is to stop on the way. Opening hours and ticket prices are in our guide to the [Piedigrotta church](/destinations/pizzo/piedigrotta-church/).

## Tropea {#tropea}

[Tropea](/destinations/tropea/) is 39 km from the resort. The way there is the coast road through Pizzo and Briatico, the former SS522 – no motorway, but sea views. The old town stands on a cliff about 60 metres above the beach. A half-day plan writes itself: a walk along the main street to the viewing terraces, the [Norman cathedral](/destinations/tropea/norman-cathedral/), the steps down to the beach and the climb to the sanctuary of [Santa Maria dell'Isola](/destinations/tropea/santa-maria-dell-isola/) on the rock opposite.

Market stalls are hung with plaits of red onions and strings of chilli peppers. What is worth taking home is covered in [What to buy in Calabria](/blog/what-to-buy-in-calabria/).

In summer the car parks by the beach fill up before noon. If you are driving, set off early or wait until the afternoon and stay for sunset – on a clear evening the cone of Stromboli shows on the horizon.

## Capo Vaticano and Zungri {#capo-vaticano}

Eleven kilometres beyond Tropea the coast pushes out to sea in a granite headland. [Capo Vaticano](/nature/capo-vaticano/) is a viewpoint by the lighthouse and a handful of small beaches under the cliff, the best known being Grotticelle. The drive from the resort takes about an hour, so the cape is best combined with Tropea in a single day out: the beach at Capo Vaticano in the morning, the town in the afternoon.

On the way back you can turn inland onto the Monte Poro plateau. First you pass Spilinga, the home of [’nduja](/cuisine/nduja/), and a few kilometres further on is Zungri. It has a rock settlement to visit – dwellings cut into the sandstone – with a small museum of rural life.

## Two or three hours out: a lake, a plane tree and hot springs {#nearby}

These places rarely make it onto excursion programmes, yet they are the closest to the resort.

**Lake Angitola (9 km).** A reservoir created in 1966, a nature reserve run by WWF since 1975 and a wetland of international importance. Water birds winter here and rest on migration – herons, cormorants, harriers and the occasional osprey. Check before you go whether the reserve is open to visitors.

**The Curinga plane tree (16 km).** In the woods above the town of Curinga grows an oriental plane thought to be around a thousand years old. The trunk is hollow, and you can step into a cavity more than three metres wide. The last stretch from the road is on foot, along a woodland path. In 2021 the tree came second in the European Tree of the Year contest. Tradition has it that it was planted by Basilian monks from the nearby hermitage of Sant'Elia, whose ruins stand a few hundred metres away.

**The Roman baths of Acconia (9 km).** The ruins of an imperial-era bathhouse stand among olive groves in the lowland part of the municipality of Curinga. We describe them on the [Baths of Acconia](/destinations/pizzo/acconia-thermal-baths/) page.

**Terme di Caronte (24 km).** Sulphur springs at 39°C in Lamezia Terme. Next to the paid spa there is a free natural pool that locals use all year round – on hot days, go in the evening.

**Gizzeria Lido (23 km).** The best-known kitesurfing spot in Calabria. A steady north-westerly blows here in the afternoons, and the schools on the beach run beginners' courses.

## Full-day trips {#day-trips}

**Scilla and Reggio Calabria.** The A2 motorway gets you to [Scilla](/destinations/scilla/) in just over an hour. The fishing quarter of [Chianalea](/destinations/scilla/chianalea/) and Ruffo Castle fill the morning, and another 25 minutes' drive brings you to [Reggio Calabria](/destinations/reggio-calabria/), where the [Riace Bronzes](/destinations/reggio-calabria/bronzi-di-riace/) stand in the National Archaeological Museum. The museum is closed on Mondays.

**Serra San Bruno.** A mountain town in the Serre range, 50 minutes from the resort. It is home to the charterhouse founded by St Bruno at the end of the 11th century – the monks live in enclosure, and visitors see the museum by its walls. In July and August it is a good escape from the heat: the beech and fir forests are noticeably cooler than the coast.

**The Aeolian Islands.** In season, excursion boats to Stromboli, Lipari and Vulcano leave from the ports of Tropea and Vibo Marina. It is a full-day outing, and the Stromboli-at-dusk version ends late in the evening.

**Cosenza.** Just over an hour north on the motorway: the [old town](/destinations/cosenza/) with its cathedral and castle, and in the modern part a pedestrian street lined with open-air sculpture.

## Getting around {#getting-around}

**Car.** This is the simplest option. Most rental companies are at Lamezia Terme airport – if you are planning several trips, you can pick up a car on arrival or hire one for two or three days mid-stay. Rules, deposits and pitfalls are covered in our guide to [car hire in Calabria](/practical/car-rental/).

**Train.** The nearest working station is Pizzo, 12 km from the resort, so you need a taxi to reach it. Regional trains running along the coast stop there: Tropea is about half an hour away, Lamezia Terme Centrale about 20 minutes. There are a dozen or so services a day, so plan your return with the timetable in hand. Vibo Valentia-Pizzo, where long-distance trains call, is a different station further inland.

**Organised excursions.** TUI's programme includes trips to Tropea and Capo Vaticano. The rep on site has the current list and prices.

**Taxi and transfer.** The ride from the airport takes about 20 minutes. For a single outing to Pizzo a return taxi makes sense; for Tropea an excursion or a one-day car hire works out cheaper.

For more route ideas, see [Things to do in Calabria: 25 places](/blog/things-to-do-in-calabria/) and the [Map of Calabria](/blog/map-of-calabria/).
